2050VISION

Natwecraft Investments is an EPC company providing complete solutions for the water, wastewater, energy and land development sectors in Zimbabwe.

What began as a power engineering and equipment hire business has grown, through the diversity of our key management team and strategic partners, into civil, mechanical and electrical engineering delivery at scale.

VISION

To be the leading civil and power engineering solutions company in Sub–Saharan Africa by year 2050.

MISSION

To achieve results by aligning our products with customer needs, conducting business with integrity and building sustainable relationships with clients, stakeholders and employees.

OBJECTIVES

Exceed customer expectations, stay cost effective without compromising quality, and grow productivity, employment and community support.
